IN PRACTICE

What this work looks like on the ground.

Two examples from recent workplace projects — listening first, then building spaces and systems people actually use.

Spaces where people can breathe

At the Walters, Kandice helped create designated lactation and meditation spaces — three separate rooms, found within the museum's existing footprint and built out with warm sage-green walls, comfortable armchairs, and soft lamplight.

She worked with a colleague on colors and swatches, partnered with Operations on the signage she designed, and shared photos of each door, floor, and location with all staff so anyone who needed a room could find it.

Feedback that leads to action

Kandice introduced monthly Employee Feedback Sessions to give staff a real platform to be heard — leadership in the room, listening to what was working, what wasn't, and what needed to change.

Items were sorted into short-term fixes and longer-term work, with an honest note up front: not everything can be fixed, but people should know their voices were heard. Good feedback isn't a suggestion box — it's a conversation that leads to action.
